# Learning from outcomes

**Inputs**: thesis, journeys, qualities and hypotheses in `specs/product.md` and `specs/product/`,
observed outcomes.
**Writes**: inbox proposals; spec edits only after the owner decides.
**Result**: a supported or inconclusive conclusion, and the next decision.

Compare observations with the thesis, qualities and hypotheses, with sources, dates, audience and
uncertainty. Passing tests and simulated users are not user evidence; missing measurement stays
unknown. Propose promotions (hypothesis to validated), revisions or retirements through the inbox,
each with a recommendation. `.prdkit/scripts/bash/retrospective.sh <change-id>` summarizes the
local ledger: execution, not product value.
